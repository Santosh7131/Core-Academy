Download Core Academy

Take the tests your teacher sets, right on your phone.

Download for Android

Needs Android 7 or newer. There is no iPhone version.

On a computer? Scan this with your phone's camera to open this page on your phone.

Install it in four steps

  1. 1

    Download the file

    Tap Download for Android. The app file is saved on your phone.

  2. 2

    Open it

    When the download finishes, tap the file in your notifications, or open it from your Downloads folder.

  3. 3

    Allow the install

    If your phone asks to allow installs from this source, say yes. Then tap Install.

  4. 4

    Sign in

    Open Core Academy and sign in with the username and PIN your teacher gave you.

Stuck on a step? Ask your teacher.

Good to know

  • Why does my phone ask?

    Core Academy is not on the Play Store, so Android checks with you before it installs the app.

  • No email needed

    You do not need an email address or a phone number to sign in. Your username and PIN are enough.

  • New versions come to you

    When a new version is ready, the app offers it to you. You do not need to come back to this page.
