How it works01

From a photo to a result for every student.

  1. 1

    Add the paper

    Take a photo of each page, choose photos from your gallery, or pick a PDF of up to 20 pages. Add the answer key too if the paper has one. No paper at hand? Describe the test and the AI writes the questions.

  2. 2

    The AI reads it

    It goes through the pages one at a time, finds each question and its options, and works out the answers. You watch the count of questions grow while it reads.

  3. 3

    Check what it is unsure about

    Questions the AI could settle are marked as ready. The rest wait in a short Needs you list: two AI checks that disagree, a figure it cannot see, a line it could not read. Tap once to choose an answer.

  4. 4

    Send it to your group

    Choose the group, set a time limit and a closing time, and publish. Students are notified when the test opens. Your home screen shows how many have submitted and how many are still writing.

  5. 5

    Students answer on their phones

    One question at a time. If you set a time limit, a timer counts down and the test submits itself when it runs out. Nothing is marked until they submit, and marks and the right answers appear after the test closes, or when you choose to show them.

  6. 6

    See who needs help

    Each student has a page with their average, their weakest chapters and the tests they missed. If an answer turns out to be wrong, fix it on the test page and everyone is marked again.

AI checking02

The AI drafts. You decide.

A printed answer key is used first. When the paper has none, two different AI models answer each question on their own.

  1. 1

    They agree and are both confident, so the question is marked.

  2. 2

    They split. A third model gives a second opinion.

  3. 3

    Still unclear. The question goes to your Needs you list, with each model's pick on the card.

What it cannot do

  • It cannot see figures or diagrams. Those questions wait for you to add a photo of the figure, or to skip them.
  • It can still be wrong. Look through the Ready list before you publish. If an answer turns out wrong later, fix it on the test page and every student is marked again.
  • Reading a page can slip on a letter, more often in some scripts. Clear printed pages in good light work best.

For students04

A username, a PIN, and the test.

  • Log in with what your tutor gives you. There is no sign-up, and no email address or phone number to hand over.

  • One question at a time. Flag one to come back to it. Every answer is saved as you choose it, even if the app closes.

  • Then the result. When the test closes, or once everyone has finished, you see your marks, every right answer, and a short list of the ones you missed.

  • More than one tutor? Join another tuition with its code and switch between them.

  • Screenshots are blocked on student phones, so questions and answers are harder to pass around.

  1. 1

    Download the file on your Android phone, then open it from your browser's downloads or from the Files app.
